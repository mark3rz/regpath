# Nimbus Health — pitch (fictional placeholder for the end-to-end sample run)

**What we do.** Nimbus is a smartphone app plus a small clip-on chest sensor that helps adolescents with moderate persistent asthma
manage their condition. The app coaches inhaler technique, tracks symptoms, and — using a machine-learned model trained on sensor
and symptom data — warns the patient and their clinician when an exacerbation looks likely in the next 72 hours.

**Users.** Patients aged 12–18 and their treating respiratory clinicians. Data flows from the sensor to the phone and then to our
cloud, where clinicians see a dashboard.

**Where we are.** Prototype sensor (custom PCB, our own enclosure). App in TestFlight. We have run a 40-patient feasibility study at
one UK children's hospital (not registered, not published). A pivotal trial is being designed with two NHS sites; endpoints are not
locked. We have not filed any patents yet — we have been advised our exacerbation-prediction method is patentable.

**Go-to-market.** UK first. Then Germany (DiGA fast-track is attractive) and the Netherlands, where our lead investor has hospital
relationships. We have a two-person team; no regulatory hire yet — we expect to use a consultancy.

**Ask.** £2.4m seed to complete the pivotal trial and reach UKCA + CE marking.
